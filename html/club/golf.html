Club Overview

The club creates a relaxed route into golf fundamentals while still giving regular players clear technique goals. Sessions usually combine warm-up drills, swing coaching, short-game repetitions, and simple target practice so members can build consistent habits.

Venue Information

  • Main venue: Practice Ground E with open hitting lanes and short-game space.
  • Check-in: Please arrive around 10 minutes early to collect equipment and group assignments.
  • Equipment: Club equipment may be shared for beginner sessions, but bringing your own set is welcome.
  • Safety: Always wait for the coach's signal before stepping into the hitting area.
  • Weather note: Strong rain or unsafe wind may shorten outdoor practice or shift the focus to technique briefing.

What We Do

  • Fundamentals: grip, stance, posture, and swing path awareness
  • Shot work: iron contact, tee shots, and distance control
  • Short game: chipping, pitching, and basic putting routine
  • Experience sessions: relaxed target drills and simple course-read discussion

Who It Is For

  • Beginners who want a low-pressure introduction to golf basics
  • Casual players improving swing consistency and control
  • Members interested in weekend outdoor sport sessions
  • Anyone who enjoys patient skill-building and technique practice

Training Plan (Sample)

  • Warm-up: mobility and swing activation (10 minutes)
  • Core block: grip, posture, and half-swing contact drills (20-25 minutes)
  • Target work: iron or tee-shot practice with coaching notes (20-30 minutes)
  • Short game: chipping or putting routine plus recap (20 minutes)

Equipment & Notes

  • Recommended: comfortable sportswear, suitable trainers, cap, and water bottle
  • Optional: your own golf glove and clubs if you already have them
  • Safety: stand clear of active swing zones and follow range etiquette
  • Timing: coming early helps with equipment setup and practice grouping
